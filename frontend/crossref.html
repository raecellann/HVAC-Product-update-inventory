Cross references

Find and backfill the custom.cross_reference metafield (UPC/GTIN) for every product in the Parts collection, using supplier sites keyed by SKU.

1. Scan and crawl

Scans the Parts collection for products missing cross_reference, then looks each SKU up at the URL you provide. Results are cached per site, so a re-run against the same template only hits the site for new SKUs.

Use {sku} (as-is), {sku_lower}, or {sku_upper} as a placeholder. Example: https://www.alltemp.ca/product/{sku_lower}/ will fetch /product/wg840596/ for SKU WG840596.

This may take a while — a Parts collection with thousands of products at ~1 sec per lookup can run for hours. Cached hits are near-instant.

2. Review results

Review the UPCs the crawler found. You can push matches to Shopify below.

Ready to update Shopify Writes the found UPCs to the custom.cross_reference metafield on each product.
